A COORDINATED APPROACH

From monitoring to informed response.

Pipeline and distributed infrastructure environments can present complex monitoring challenges. Depending on the systems selected, cameras, sensors, analytics and operator workflows may help identify events that need review.

Technology should complement established operating procedures and trained human decision-makers. Actual system capabilities and response arrangements must be confirmed for each project.

01

Cameras & Sensors

Monitor critical infrastructure, gathering visual and environmental data to help security teams maintain awareness of site conditions and activities.

↓
02

AI Analysis

AI-powered analytics process available camera and sensor data to identify unusual patterns, recognize relevant activity, and highlight events that may require further investigation.

↓
03

Threat Detection

The system identifies activity that matches configured security indicators, helping teams detect potential threats and prioritize events for assessment.

↓
04

Security Alert

When a potential threat is detected, the system generates an alert to notify designated security personnel, enabling timely review and appropriate escalation.

↓
05

Human Verification

Trained security personnel assess the alert, review available evidence, and determine whether the event represents a genuine threat before taking further action.

↓
06

Security Response

Following verification, the appropriate response procedures are initiated, coordinating relevant personnel and supporting a timely, structured response in line with established protocols.
